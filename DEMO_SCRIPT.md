# KathaVaani — 2-3 Minute Demo Video Script

> **The Great Indian AI Internship Challenge**  
> *Video Duration: ~2 minutes 30 seconds*  
> *Theme: The Vintage Indian Audiobook Parlour*

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] The Hook & Problem
* **Visual**: Camera or screen recording opens on a person gazing at dense regional literature (e.g. a Telugu or Hindi book on screen or desk). Ambient warm lighting.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Millions of us love listening to stories in our mother tongue. But while English audiobooks are everywhere, our rich regional Indian literature—in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, and Bengali—remains locked away in dense text. What if you could walk into an old antique bookshop, hand over any manuscript, and have it read aloud in its true native voice?"*

---

### [0:25 - 0:50] Screen 1 & Screen 2: The Entrance & The Counter
* **Visual**: Browser opens `http://127.0.0.1:8000`. The warm vintage sign of **KathaVaani** glows. The user clicks **"Enter the Bookshop 🗝️"**. The screen performs a 3D page curl transition to **The Counter**.
* **Action**: User shows the drag-and-drop counter, then clicks the Telugu classic on the antique shelf: **"బంగారు మామిడిపండ్లు" (Tenali Ramakrishna)**.
* **Visual**: The manuscript docket stamps open instantly: *"Detected: Telugu Manuscript • 1 Page • Selectable Text"*.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Meet KathaVaani. Step through the oak doors into our vintage parlour. At the front counter, you can drop any PDF or TXT book, or pluck a classic from our library. Behind the scenes, PyMuPDF extracts the clean text layer page by page."*

---

### [0:50 - 1:20] Screen 3 & Screen 4: Reading Nook & Narrator Studio
* **Visual**: User clicks **"Inspect Pages & Language ⟶"**. Page turn flips to **The Reading Nook**.
* **Action**: User adjusts the page scope slider, then scrolls along the **Spine Shelf**—clicking the **Telugu (తెలుగు)** spine, which pulls forward with a golden glow.
* **Action**: Flips to **Narrator Studio**. Shows **Kavya** and **Kabir**.
* **Action**: User clicks **"Hear Voice Preview"** on Kavya. A warm, crystal-clear 2-second Telugu voice line plays directly:
  *(Audio: "కథావాణి పాత పుస్తకాల దుకాణానికి స్వాగతం...")*
* **Voiceover**: 
  > *"In the Reading Nook, pick your chapter scope and pull your book's native tongue from the spine shelf. In the Narrator Studio, choose between Kavya, our lyrical storyteller, and Kabir, our resonant sage. Every voice is powered by Gnani Timbre v2.5—built ground-up for Indian languages."*

---

### [1:20 - 1:55] Screen 5 & Screen 6: The Binding Station & The Library
* **Visual**: User clicks **"Bind Your Audiobook 🧵"**. Flips to **The Binding Station**.
* **Action**: The antique press animation pulses. Status lines update live:
  * *"Splitting text at natural sentence pauses (`.` `!` `।`)..."*
  * *"Requesting synthesis from Gnani Timbre v2.5..."*
  * *"Stitching audio reels with antique pauses..."*
* **Visual**: Flips to **The Library**. The gramophone vinyl spins, the tonearm lowers, and authentic Telugu narration fills the room. Beside the vinyl player, the Telugu manuscript displays on parchment paper.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Our pipeline decomposes the text into natural sentence chunks, synthesizes each chunk via Gnani's REST API, and stitches them with 250ms breathing pauses into a continuous master MP3. All chunks are cryptographically cached on disk, making repeated replays cost zero credits."*

---

### [1:55 - 2:30] Second Language Demo, Download & Impact
* **Action**: User clicks **"Download MP3 💾"** (showing the downloaded audiobook file), then quickly demonstrates switching to **Hindi (पंच परमेश्वर by Munshi Premchand)** or **English**, proving polyglot capability.
* **Visual**: Split screen highlighting the 10 supported Indian languages and the GitHub / submission link.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > *"With full support for 10 Indian languages, KathaVaani bridges the gap between written heritage and modern listening—for students, commuters, and visually impaired readers alike. KathaVaani: Giving India's stories their authentic voice."*
