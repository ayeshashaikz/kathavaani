# KathaVaani (कथावाणी) — Narrator Character Dossier & Voice Profiles
*Powered by Gnani Timbre v2.5 Text-to-Speech*

---

## 1. Overview & Concept
KathaVaani breathes acoustic life into written literature using two curated narrator personas: **Kavya (Feminine Voice)** and **Kabir (Masculine Voice)**. Each character is engineered to embody distinct acoustic dynamics, storytelling cadence, and emotional warmth across 10 major Indian languages. The literature is spoken strictly in its native mother tongue without any artificial translation step.

---

## 2. Character Profile: Kavya (काव्या / కావ్య)

![Kavya](images/character1.jpg)

* **Role**: Feminine Storyteller & Lyricist Persona
* **Underlying Gnani Voice**: `Nalini` (Model: `timbre-v2.5`)
* **Archetype**: Empathetic, lyrical storyteller with nuanced emotional pacing
* **Acoustic Profile**: Warm mid-range register, gentle breath modulation, melodic intonation on conjunct syllables, fluid sentence flow.
* **Ideal Literary Genres**: Poetry, character-driven fiction, folklore, memoirs, children's fables, and emotional drama.
* **Signature Quote**: *"Every word holds a fragrance; I merely allow it to take flight into the open air."*

---

## 3. Character Profile: Kabir (कबीर / కబీర్)

![Kabir](images/character2.jpg)

* **Role**: Masculine Storyteller & Sage Persona
* **Underlying Gnani Voice**: `Deepak` (Model: `timbre-v2.5`)
* **Archetype**: Grounded, resonant elder narrator with calm, timeless authority
* **Acoustic Profile**: Baritone depth, deliberate cadence, crisp articulation of plosives, reflective pauses between sentences.
* **Ideal Literary Genres**: Historical narratives, philosophical essays, classic epics, biographies, crime fiction, and reflective non-fiction.
* **Signature Quote**: *"Time wears away stone, but a story well-spoken remains unyielding."*
